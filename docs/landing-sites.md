# Landing-site markers

The viewer shows successful Apollo and robotic landings, plus Shackleton crater.
Crashes, unconfirmed attempts, and limited missions (Luna 23, Odysseus, Athena)
are excluded. The broader source catalog stays available in the data module.
Use the grouped **Go to location** menu to visit a
site; a note below the menu shows the operator, year, outcome, uncertainty, and
coordinate source for robotic missions.

The **Mission markers** flag buttons toggle United States, Soviet Union,
China, India, and Japan markers independently. Countries without visible sites
have no button. All start enabled;
dimmed buttons mean hidden. These
filters affect labels and dots, including Apollo under the US button;
Shackleton and the physical Apollo models are unaffected.

Coordinates were checked against the [NASA NSSDCA spacecraft-on-the-Moon catalog](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar/lunar_artifact_impacts.html)
on 2026-10-18. They use planetocentric latitude and signed east longitude. The
catalog mixes historical estimates and LROC-localized hardware: decimal places
alone do not establish accuracy. Luna 9/13 and several failed early missions
retain approximate coordinates. Surveyor 4 and the proposed Luna 18 impact
location remain unconfirmed. New candidate locations have not been treated as
confirmed replacements.

- White dots: Apollo.
- Blue dots: successful robotic landings.
- Gold dot: Shackleton crater.

Luna 17/21 and Chang’e 3/4 mark the lander, not the rover’s later position.
This is a lander catalog: it does not include planned future landings,
spacecraft that never reached the Moon, booster impacts, or every deliberate
orbiter/probe impact. Apollo Lunar Module models remain specific to Apollo.

Mission-outcome context: [NASA IM-2 mission conclusion](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-receives-some-data-before-intuitive-machines-ends-lunar-mission/),
[NASA Odysseus update](https://www.nasa.gov/blogs/artemis/2024/02/28/nasa-intuitive-machines-share-images-from-the-moon-provide-science-updates/).
